Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Grillz Direct ("we", "us") makes custom teeth jewellery for customers in Ireland and the United Kingdom. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

Who we are

Grillz Direct is the data controller for the information described here. You can contact us about anything in this policy at prodmizzdirect@gmail.com or on Instagram at @grillzdirect.

What we collect

  • When you place an order: your name, email address, phone number and shipping address, plus the details of your order (teeth, metal, style and amounts paid). Our payment provider, Stripe, collects your payment details directly — we never see or store your full card number.
  • When you contact us: your name, email address and whatever you write in your message.
  • Mould impressions: the dental mould you send us so we can make your grillz fit.
  • On your own device: the site remembers your light/dark mode choice, your currency and the items in your order using your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To make, deliver and support your order, and to take your deposit and balance — this is necessary to carry out our contract with you.
  • To reply to your messages and quote for custom designs — our legitimate interest in running the business, or steps you ask for before entering a contract.
  • To keep financial records — a legal obligation under tax and accounting law.

We don't sell your information or use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe processes payments and receives the details you enter at checkout. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Netlify hosts this website and receives contact-form messages. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which see your IP address.
  • Delivery companies receive your name and address when we post your mould kit or finished grillz.

Some of these providers process data outside the EU and UK. Where they do, they rely on approved safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for six years to meet tax and accounting requirements. Contact messages are kept for up to two years unless they relate to an order. Mould impressions are kept until your order is complete and any remake period has passed, then destroyed.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to another provider. Email us and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland or the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.
