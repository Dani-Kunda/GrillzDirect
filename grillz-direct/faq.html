FAQ & Care

Everything you need to know before, during and after your order.

Ordering

How do I get the right fit?

After your deposit, we send you a mould kit with step-by-step instructions. You take the impression at home and send it back to us.

Are prices per arch?

Yes — the price list covers one arch (top or bottom). For both, add each arch separately or use the build page.

How long does it take?

Most sets are ready within a few weeks of receiving your mould. Custom designs can take longer; we'll give you a timeline with your quote.

Can I get a custom design?

Absolutely. Custom pieces are priced as TBD — send us your idea through the contact page or on Instagram and we'll quote you.

Can I get a refund?

Because each piece is made to your mould, deposits are non-refundable once work has started. Message us if there's an issue with your set.

Aftercare

Cleaning

Rinse after each wear and brush gently with a soft toothbrush and mild soap. Dry fully before storing.

Eating & drinking

Take your grillz out before eating. Avoid wearing them with sugary or acidic drinks.

Storage

Keep them in their case, away from moisture and other jewellery that might scratch them.

Oral hygiene

Brush and floss your own teeth before putting your grillz in, and don't sleep in them.
